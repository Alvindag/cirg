How to Report — Raising Concerns & Whistleblowing
Know exactly how to raise a concern, report unethical behaviour, and use DAS Plc's confidential reporting channels — without fear.

🗣️ Your Duty to Report

DAS Plc cannot maintain high ethical standards without employees who speak up. You are expected to report genuine concerns — even when it feels uncomfortable.

Concerns you are expected to report include:

🔴Fraud — including financial misrepresentation, false invoices, or expense abuse
🔴Theft — of company property, cash, or data
🔴Harassment or discrimination against any employee
🔴Non-compliance with regulations, legislation, policies, or procedures
🔴Misrepresentation — in reports, accounts, or customer/supplier dealings
🔴Safety violations that put employees or the public at risk
🔴Any breach of this Business Ethics Policy

📞 How to Raise a Concern — Step by Step

Step 1 — First Instance

Advise the Head of Human Resources or Head of Corporate Affairs. You may do this anonymously if you wish.

To protect confidentiality: do not discuss your concern with colleagues or other third parties unless specifically authorised or legally required to do so.

Step 2 — If You Are Dissatisfied or the Matter Is Serious

Use the Confidential Reporting Hotline. This line is available for employees to raise concerns for reporting, investigation, and resolution by the CEO and/or Board.

DAS Plc Confidential Reporting Hotline
0241640540
Available to all DAS Plc employees

🛡️ You Are Protected When You Report

DAS Plc is committed to protecting employees who raise genuine concerns in good faith.

✔Your concern will be given due consideration
✔It will be reviewed objectively and impartially
✔Your rights will be protected throughout the investigation
✔You may report anonymously if you wish
✔Retaliation against someone who reports a genuine concern is itself a disciplinary offence

⚖️ Good Judgement When Policy Doesn't Cover Everything

This policy cannot anticipate every situation. You are expected to exercise good ethical judgement even when specific rules do not exist for a situation. When in doubt:

  • Ask: "Would I be comfortable if DAS Plc management saw exactly what I am doing and why?"
  • If the answer is no — stop and seek guidance from the Head of Corporate Affairs or Legal Counsel
