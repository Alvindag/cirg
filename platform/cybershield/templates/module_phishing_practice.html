👣 What To Do — Step-by-Step

Memorise this sequence. It works the same way whether you're on desktop or web.

🛑1
STOP — don't click anything inside it
No links, no attachments, no "unsubscribe". Just select the email in the list — do not open it if you can help it.
🖱️2
SELECT the email once
A single click in the message list is enough. You do not need to open it to report it.
🚩3
Click "Report" (ribbon) or "…" → Report (web)
Exactly where you saw on the previous screen. Same icon every time — train your eye to spot the little flag/megaphone icon.
🎣4
Choose "Phishing" — not "Junk"
Junk only filters your own inbox. Phishing notifies Microsoft's security team and flags it in DAS Plc's ISMS Phishing Intelligence log.
✅5
Confirm in the dialog box
Click "Report" to confirm. Outlook removes the email from your inbox automatically.
📞6
If it looked targeted at you specifically, also call IT — Ext. 204
Emails using your real name, job title, or referencing an actual DAS Plc system deserve a phone call too — it speeds up the investigation.
