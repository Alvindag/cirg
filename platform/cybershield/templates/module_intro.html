Welcome to DAS Plc Security
Why cybersecurity matters at a pharmaceutical manufacturing company — Ghana DPA 2012, Ghana FDA, and what a breach really costs DAS Plc

🏭 About DAS Plc

DAS Plc (Dannex Ayrton Starwin Plc) is a pharmaceutical manufacturing group.

Our IT systems — from Business Central ERP to Active Directory and Microsoft 365 — handle sensitive financial data, manufacturing records, supplier contracts, and production formulas every single day. This makes us a high-value target for cybercriminals.

  • 200+ employee workstations across DAS Plc
  • Business Central ERP handling production planning and financial transactions
  • Microsoft 365 (Exchange, Teams, SharePoint) for all communications
  • Regulated under Ghana FDA for pharmaceutical manufacturing data integrity
  • Subject to Ghana Data Protection Act 2012 for employee and supplier data
  • Active Directory domain: dasplcgh.com managing all user accounts

⚠️ Why This Training Matters

Over 90% of successful cyberattacks begin with a human — not a technical vulnerability. An employee clicking one wrong link can lead to:

  • Ransomware encrypting all DAS Plc files — production stops across DAS Plc
  • Financial fraud through Business Email Compromise (BEC) where fake supplier invoices approved in Business Central
  • Data breach exposing confidential supplier contracts and employee personal information
  • Ghana FDA investigation and potential licence suspension for manufacturing record tampering
  • Regulatory fines under the Ghana Data Protection Act 2012
  • Theft of proprietary batch formulas and manufacturing IP worth millions of Ghana cedis
  • Reputational damage to the DAS Plc brand and its products across Ghana

You are the most important security control in DAS Plc.

📂 DAS Plc Data Classification

Not all data is equal. Know how to handle each level — because mishandling data is itself a security incident:

LevelExamples at DAS PlcRequired Handling
Public Product catalogues, website content, press releases May be shared externally
Internal Use Internal policies, meeting minutes, staff announcements Internal only, not for external sharing
Confidential Financial reports, supplier contracts, HR employment data, Business Central data Need-to-know basis, encrypted when shared
Restricted Batch manufacturing formulas, QC test records, production master data, AD credentials, Ghana FDA regulatory submissions Strictly controlled. Ghana FDA compliance required. Never emailed unencrypted

✅ Your Security Responsibilities

  • Report suspicious emails, calls or visitors to IT immediately. Do not wait
  • Never share your Active Directory password with anyone — including IT staff
  • Lock your workstation when leaving your desk (Windows + L)
  • Do not install any software from USB or the internet without IT approval
  • Report lost or stolen devices within 1 hour to IT
  • Complete annual cybersecurity refresher training every year
  • Read and sign the DAS Plc Acceptable Use Policy IT22-001
  • Never photograph or share screens showing Restricted or Confidential data
