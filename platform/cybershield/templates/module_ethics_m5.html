Your Rights & Responsibilities as a DAS Employee
Understand what DAS Plc commits to you as an employee — and what you owe to your colleagues and the organisation in return.

⚖️ Labour Act 651 — Your Rights at DAS Plc

DAS Plc is committed to full compliance with Ghana's Labour Act 651. This means:

✔Your employment contract reflects your correct terms and conditions
✔You are entitled to fair pay and agreed benefits
✔You are entitled to annual leave, maternity/paternity provisions, and sick leave as stipulated in Labour Act 651
✔You cannot be dismissed without due process
✔You have the right to join a lawful trade union

If you believe your rights under Labour Act 651 are being violated, raise it with the Head of Human Resources or the confidential hotline.

🚫 Harassment — Zero Tolerance

Harassment includes any unwanted behaviour that a person finds intimidating, upsetting, embarrassing, humiliating, or offensive. This includes racial, sexual, and any other form of harassment.

⚠ DAS Plc's Position

Harassment — in any form — is unacceptable and will not be countenanced under any circumstances.

  • If you experience harassment, raise it with the Head of Human Resources who will arrange an impartial investigation without delay
  • If you witness harassment, you are expected to report it
  • Any threat of harassment should also be reported — you do not have to wait for an incident to occur

🛡 Protection Against Retaliation

DAS Plc will protect employees who raise genuine concerns about harassment. Retaliation against anyone who reports harassment is itself a disciplinary offence.

🌍 Equal Opportunity at DAS Plc

DAS Plc values all employees for their contribution. Advancement within DAS Plc will be based solely on:

  • Performance — what you deliver
  • Ability — what you are capable of
  • Aptitude — your potential to grow

Decisions will never be influenced by gender, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, nationality, or any other characteristic unrelated to the job.

📌 Health & Safety

DAS Plc places the highest priority on protecting health and safety — particularly for employees working in high-risk production environments at DAS Plc.

  • Risks are continuously evaluated, mitigated, and managed
  • Safety procedures must be followed without exception
  • Leaders must model the highest safety standards
  • Health and safety is the first consideration in everything DAS Plc does

Report any unsafe condition to your supervisor or Health & Safety officer immediately.

🌱 DAS Plc as a Good Corporate Citizen

DAS Plc operates in communities across Ghana. We take our responsibility to those communities seriously.

Environment

  • DAS Plc targets annual reductions in carbon intensity, waste, water use, vehicle emissions, and energy consumption
  • Every employee has a role — avoid waste, follow waste disposal procedures, report environmental violations

Community & CSR

  • DAS Plc supports charitable social initiatives through fund-raising, community project sponsorship, and voluntary work
  • Our operations are conducted with respect and consideration for local communities
  • We minimise disturbance to communities surrounding our production sites

Pre-Employment Screening

DAS Plc applies rigorous pre-employment screening to protect its employees, customers, and the integrity of its pharmaceutical products. This is a requirement of the business — not optional.
