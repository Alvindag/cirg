Smishing & Vishing
Phishing via SMS and phone calls — how criminals use your mobile phone to steal credentials and access DAS Plc systems.

📱 What Is Smishing?

Smishing = SMS + Phishing. Attackers send text messages pretending to be your bank, HR, or IT.

  • Fake HR SMS: "Update your bank details for April payroll — link expires tonight"
  • Fake IT SMS: "Your M365 password expires in 2 hours. Reset here: das-reset.net"
  • Fake BC SMS: "Business Central payment requires your approval — click to approve"
  • Fake prize: "You have won GHS 5,000! Claim at: bit.ly/daswin"

DAS Plc HR and IT will NEVER send bank detail or password requests via SMS. BC approvals are done inside BC only — never via SMS links.

📞 What Is Vishing?

Vishing = Voice + Phishing. Attackers call pretending to be IT, Microsoft, your bank, or a senior manager to pressure you into giving access or information.

  • "This is Microsoft Support — your computer has been flagged with a virus"
  • "This is the CEO's office — process an urgent transfer in Business Central now"
  • "This is IT — we need your login to migrate your account"
  • "This is Kaspersky — we need remote access to fix your antivirus"

IT staff will NEVER call you and ask for your password. Ever.
