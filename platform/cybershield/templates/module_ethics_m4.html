MODULE 4 OF 6 · BUSINESS ETHICS
Customers, Suppliers & Fair Competition
Understand how DAS Plc expects you to deal with customers, suppliers, and competitors — with fairness, honesty, and integrity.

🤝 Treatment of Customers

Mutual trust and confidence between DAS Plc and our customers is vital. The pharmaceutical industry depends on it.

✅ What DAS Plc Expects

  • Service excellence and value for money — consistently, not occasionally
  • Open and clear communication — accurate understanding of what the customer expects and what DAS Plc has committed to
  • Anticipating needs — not just meeting current requirements but understanding changing requirements
  • Honest feedback — if you become aware of practices in a customer's business that conflict with good industry practice, raise it

📌 Important

DAS Plc is obliged to dialogue with clients if honouring any request would compromise DAS Plc's own ethical standards. You must escalate — not comply silently — if a customer request crosses an ethical line.

🏭 Treatment of Suppliers

DAS Plc's suppliers provide the raw materials, equipment, and services that keep production running. They are entitled to fair treatment.

DAS Plc's Commitment to Suppliers
Pay suppliers in accordance with agreed terms of trade — no deliberate payment delays
Set high ethical standards for suppliers — our ethics policy extends to our supply chain
Select suppliers based solely on quality, performance, price, and value — never on inducements
Treat all potential suppliers equally and fairly in procurement processes

⚠ Procurement Reminder

Employees involved in procurement have heightened responsibilities. Any gifts, inducements, or unusual requests from suppliers must be declared immediately to your supervisor and HR.

🏆 Fair Competition

DAS Plc competes vigorously in the Ghanaian pharmaceutical market — but always fairly and ethically.

✅ Acceptable Competitive Behaviour

  • Building competitive advantage through product quality, innovation, and service
  • Monitoring publicly available competitor information (public reports, media, etc.)
  • Competing on price, value, and performance

❌ Strictly Prohibited

  • Sharing confidential information when in contact with competitors
  • Attempting to obtain competitors' trade secrets or confidential information improperly
  • Discussing pricing information with competitors — even indirectly — as this may constitute price-fixing under Ghanaian law
  • Any conduct that conflicts with applicable competition laws

If a competitor approaches you at a conference, trade event, or informally — and begins discussing pricing or market strategy — you must disengage and report it to the Head of Corporate Affairs.

📢 External Reporting & Media

DAS Plc may be required to make statements or provide reports to regulatory bodies, government agencies, or the media.

⚠ Key Rules

  • All statements and reports must be correct, timely, and not misleading
  • Senior management must be made aware of any sensitive disclosure before it is made
  • Only authorised spokespersons may represent DAS Plc in media or external relations
  • All media enquiries must be referred to the company's media relations expert — do not respond directly

🔴 Important — If a Journalist Contacts You

Do not comment. Do not speculate. Do not say "no comment" and walk away. The correct response is: "All media enquiries should go through our communications team. I'll pass your details along." Then report it to your line manager immediately.
