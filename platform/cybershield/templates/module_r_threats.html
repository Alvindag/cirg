New Threats 2025/2026
The threat landscape has evolved. Here's what's new and how it specifically affects DAS Plc employees.

🤖 AI-Generated Phishing — The New Reality

Traditional phishing detection relied on spotting spelling mistakes and poor grammar. In 2025, AI tools allow attackers to generate grammatically perfect, contextually relevant, personalised phishing emails at scale.

  • AI scans your LinkedIn profile and crafts emails referencing your actual job title and recent projects
  • AI-generated emails pass traditional spam filters — the language is indistinguishable from real correspondence
  • Deepfake voice technology enables convincing vishing calls mimicking colleagues
  • AI chatbots conduct multi-turn phishing conversations via WhatsApp or Teams

💡 New defence strategy: Verify unexpected requests through a SEPARATE communication channel. If you get a suspicious Teams message from the MD, call their direct line — don't rely on the same channel.

📱 Quishing — QR Code Phishing

Quishing uses malicious QR codes embedded in emails, printed flyers, fake invoices, or posters to redirect victims to phishing sites.

QR codes are dangerous because:

  • You cannot see the URL before scanning — unlike a hyperlink you can hover over
  • Many email security gateways do not scan QR codes embedded in images
  • Attackers place fake QR stickers over legitimate ones in public spaces
  • Common scenarios: fake invoice QR codes, "scan to access the meeting" links, "verify your account" codes
DAS Plc Rule: Never scan a QR code in an unsolicited email or document without verifying with IT (Ext. 204) first. Legitimate DAS Plc communications will not require you to scan QR codes.

🏭 OT Attacks — Pharmaceutical Sector Risk

Operational Technology (OT) attacks target the computerised systems controlling manufacturing processes. For DAS Plc, this means:

  • Business Central ERP — attackers could manipulate production batch data or financial records
  • Production control systems — interference with drug manufacturing processes across DAS Plc
  • Quality control data — falsification of batch records (critical under Ghana FDA)

The pharmaceutical sector was the second most targeted industry by ransomware groups in 2024/2025.

🔴 DAS Plc Specific Risk: NAV 2016 is running on End-of-Life Windows Server 2016 infrastructure. This combination is a known high-value target. Patch management and access control are critical.
