Password Hygiene & MFA
Strong passwords and Multi-Factor Authentication stop the majority of cyber attacks. Learn the DAS Plc AD policy and test your password strength live.

🔑 Why Password Hygiene Matters

Weak passwords are one of the top causes of data breaches. Once an attacker has your Active Directory credentials, they have access to everything your account can reach — Business Central, SharePoint, Teams, email, and network drives.

RuleRequirement

🔐 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

MFA adds a second layer of security beyond your password. Even if an attacker steals your password, they cannot log in without the second factor.

  • Something you know — your password
  • Something you have — your phone (Microsoft Authenticator app OTP)
  • Something you are — fingerprint or face ID (future rollout)

📢 DAS Plc MFA Rollout (R-2026-0001): MFA is being enabled for all Microsoft 365 accounts. You will receive setup instructions from IT. The process takes 5 minutes using the Microsoft Authenticator app.

IMPORTANT: If you receive an MFA approval request that YOU did not initiate — DENY it immediately and call IT on Ext. 204. This means someone has your password and is trying to log in right now.
