Records, Confidentiality & Conflicts of Interest
Learn your obligations around accurate record-keeping, protecting DAS Plc's confidential information, and avoiding conflicts of interest.

📂 Record-Keeping — Your Obligations

Accurate records protect DAS Plc legally, financially, and reputationally. Every employee who handles accounts, invoices, or records has these obligations:

✅ What You Must Do

  • All accounts, invoices, and records must be strictly accurate and complete
  • All accounting documentation must clearly identify the true nature of business transactions, assets, and liabilities
  • All reporting must be accurate and compliant with accounting standards, policies, and procedures
  • Declare all hospitality or gifts by logging details into Business Central (NAV) or keeping a written record

❌ What Is Expressly Prohibited

  • No "off-book" accounts — accounts must not be kept off-book to hide improper payments
  • No false or incomplete entries — no record or entry may be suppressed or falsified
  • No misrepresentation — employees must not materially misstate management information for personal gain or any other reason

🔒 Confidential Information — What It Means at DAS Plc

As a DAS Plc employee, you have access to information that belongs to the company. This includes:

  • Manufacturing formulas and production processes
  • Supplier contracts and pricing
  • Customer data and business plans
  • Financial records and forecasts
  • HR and personnel information
  • IT systems, infrastructure, and security details

📌 The Rule

You must not use confidential information for personal gain or for any purpose other than advancing DAS Plc's interests.

Disclosure to any third party — during or after employment — is not permitted unless:

  • It has been appropriately authorised
  • It is for a legitimate business reason
  • The information is being communicated securely

Note: Confidential information includes anything described as confidential or obviously confidential from context. It does not include information already in the public domain or information required by law to be disclosed.

⚖️ Conflicts of Interest — What to Watch For

A conflict of interest occurs when your personal interests could influence — or appear to influence — your professional decisions at DAS Plc.

Examples of Conflicts of Interest

🔴Owning shares in or having a financial interest in a DAS Plc supplier or competitor
🔴Approving a contract awarded to a company owned by a family member
🔴Using DAS Plc resources, time, or information to run a personal business
🔴Making hiring decisions that favour a friend or relative
🔴Receiving commissions or side payments from suppliers for decisions you influence

✅ What You Must Do If a Conflict Exists

  1. Disclose it to the appropriate level of management immediately
  2. Do not proceed with the activity until you receive written confirmation that it does not constitute a conflict
  3. If the conflict is confirmed — terminate the activity

Even activities that appear to present a conflict must be avoided or disclosed. Perception matters as much as reality.
