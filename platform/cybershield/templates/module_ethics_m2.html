Bribery, Corruption & Gifts — The Rules
Understand what constitutes bribery and corruption at DAS Plc, when gifts are acceptable, and how to declare them properly.

🚫 DAS Plc's Position on Bribery & Corruption

DAS Plc is absolutely opposed to bribery and corruption in any form. This means:

  • No payments, gifts, or inducements made to influence someone to act improperly — this is expressly prohibited
  • No payments to public officials to influence their performance of duty (unless legally required by written law)
  • All sales and purchases are made solely on quality, performance, price, and value — never on the basis of giving or receiving inducements

Consequences: Violations will be investigated and may result in disciplinary action including summary dismissal. Criminal proceedings may also follow.

🎁 When Are Gifts Acceptable?

Not all gifts are prohibited. A gift is acceptable only when it meets ALL of these conditions:

✅ Acceptable Gift Criteria

✔Modest in value — not disproportionate to the business relationship
✔Infrequent — not a regular occurrence that could imply ongoing inducement
✔Appropriate to the nature of the business relationship
✔Does not create any appearance of impropriety
✔Declared to the appropriate person (see below)

❌ Always Prohibited

  • No cash gifts — cash gifts must never be given or received under any circumstances
  • Any gift given or received with intent to influence a procurement decision — must be declared and returned. If return is impossible, it is donated to charity.

📝 How to Declare a Gift — DAS Gift Declaration Rules

Every acceptable gift received must be declared. Here is who to declare to:

If you are…Declare to…
EXCO memberThe Chief Executive Officer
All other employeesThe Head of Human Resources
Procurement roleGifts come under extra scrutiny — declare immediately; return if intent to influence is suspected

How to declare: Log the gift details into the appropriate section in NAV / Business Central, or complete the Declaration of Gifts form. This applies whether you accepted the gift or not.

⚠ DAS Plc reserves the right to remove any undeclared gift from the employee, and for it to be raffled for charity.
