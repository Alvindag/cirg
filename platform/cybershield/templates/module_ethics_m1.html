📋 What Is the Business Ethics Policy?

DAS Plc's Business Ethics Policy (Ref: DAS/BEP/005 V5) sets the minimum standards of operation for every employee across DAS Plc.

It covers how we deal with customers, suppliers, competitors, each other, and the public — based on the DAS Values.

🌟 The DAS Values

IntegrityWe are honest and transparent in everything we do
RespectWe treat every person — staff, customer, supplier — with dignity
ExcellenceWe set high standards and continuously improve
AccountabilityWe own our actions and their consequences
TeamworkWe collaborate and support each other across DAS Plc

👥 Who Does This Policy Apply To?

This policy applies to every employee at every level across DAS Plc from production floor staff to the Executive Committee.

✅ What the Policy Requires

  • All DAS senior managers must communicate this policy annually to their departments
  • The Executive, managers, and officers must personally endorse and confirm compliance within their areas
  • All new employment contracts must include the Business Ethics Policy — signed, filed, and auditable
  • The policy is reviewed annually to stay current
  • Strict adherence is a condition of employment at DAS Plc

⚠ Consequences of Non-Compliance

Any action that deliberately or recklessly breaches this policy may result in:

  • Formal disciplinary action
  • Summary dismissal (immediate termination)
  • Criminal proceedings, where appropriate
